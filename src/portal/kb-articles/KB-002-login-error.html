KB-002: Resolving Login Authentication Errors

Article ID: KB-002

Last Updated: May 2026

Applies To: BestVPN v2.0+

Symptoms

Users may experience one or more of the following issues:

  • "Authentication failed" error message
  • "Invalid credentials" even with correct username/password
  • "Session expired" immediately after login
  • Login screen keeps reappearing after entering credentials

Cause

This issue typically occurs due to:

  • Expired or invalid subscription
  • Cached credentials corruption
  • System clock out of sync (affects token validation)
  • Account locked due to multiple failed attempts

Resolution

  1. Verify your subscription status
    Log in to account.bestvpn.com to check your subscription is active.
  2. Reset your password
    Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to reset your credentials.
  3. Clear cached credentials
    Go to BestVPN > Preferences > Account > Sign Out, then sign in again.
  4. Sync your system clock
    Open System Preferences > Date & Time > Check "Set date and time automatically"
  5. Clear Keychain entries
    Open Keychain Access, search for "BestVPN", and delete any stored entries. Then try logging in again.
  6. Wait 15 minutes if account is locked
    After 5 failed login attempts, accounts are temporarily locked for security.
Tip: Enterprise users should contact their IT administrator if the issue persists, as your organization may have specific authentication policies.
Note: Never share your login credentials. BestVPN support will never ask for your password.
